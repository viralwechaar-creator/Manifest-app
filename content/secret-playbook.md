# The Secret: Coach Playbook

Distilled once from *The Secret* by Rhonda Byrne (2006). Written in my own words. It is a knowledge base for the app's AI coach, not a copy of the book.

## Part 1. The book's core framework

**P1. Law of attraction.** Like attracts like — dominant thoughts act like a signal, and life brings back matching circumstances.
**P2. Focus on what you want.** State wants in positive form.
**P3. Now is the point of power.** You can change your thinking now.
**P4. Feelings are your feedback.** Watch how you feel, not every thought.
**P5. Lift your feeling deliberately.**

## Part 2. Ask, Believe, Receive
1. Ask — get clear on exactly what you want.
2. Believe — act, speak, think as if it's already yours.
3. Receive — feel now the way you'll feel once you have it.

## Part 3. Practices
Clarity, feeling check, gratitude, visualization, act as if, evening revision, create the day in advance, quiet the mind, appreciation list, generous giving.

## Part 4. By life area
Money, Relationships, Health, The world, You, Life — each with book-specific framing.

## Part 5. Coach layer (NOT in the book)
Pairs each mindset practice with a concrete real-world action (what/when/how long/done-when).

## Part 6. Guardrails (always)
Never present as proven fact, never blame the user for tragedy, never advise stopping medical treatment, no investment advice, never shame feelings, drop to crisis response on self-harm mentions, never quote long passages.
